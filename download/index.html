Install Reno App

Track renovation payments, vendors, budget and bills. Your data stays in your own OneDrive — sign in with your Microsoft account.

📱 iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open Reno App below.
  3. Tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  4. Open Reno App from your Home Screen and sign in once.
Open Reno App

💻 Mac

  1. Download the installer below and open it.
  2. Drag Reno App into Applications.
  3. First time only: right-click Reno App → Open → Open.
  4. Sign in with your Microsoft account.
⬇︎ Download for Mac (.dmg) Or the .pkg installer Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 12 or newer. macOS warns once because the app isn't from the App Store.

🖥️ Windows, Android or any browser

Just open Reno App in your browser. In Chrome or Edge you can choose Install app from the menu.
